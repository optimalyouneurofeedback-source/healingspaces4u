Insurance

  • MODA / ODS

Other ways to pay

  • Private pay
  • Sliding fee scale
  • HSA
  • Credit card: Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express

Neurofeedback isn't billed to insurance. See neurofeedback pricing.

Tip: Call the number on your insurance card and ask about your "outpatient mental health" benefits, including any copay or deductible. I'm happy to help you sort it out during your free consult.

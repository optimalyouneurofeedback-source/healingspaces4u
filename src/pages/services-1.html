How it works

Relax, listen, and let your brain do the work

You sit back in a comfortable chair with sensors that simply listen. Music plays, and brief pauses give your brain real-time feedback so it can practice settling into a calmer, more flexible state.

Nothing is sent into the brain. I use NeurOptimal® dynamical neurofeedback. Sessions last about 33 minutes.

Alongside therapy: neurofeedback calms the central nervous system, which can make tough emotions easier to tolerate in and out of sessions. It pairs especially well with EMDR.

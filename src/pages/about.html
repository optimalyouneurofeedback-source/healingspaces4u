Sarita E. Trawick, LCSW

Hi, I'm Sarita

I'm a licensed clinical social worker (LCSW) and have worked in mental health for more than 20 years.

I offer counseling to Black/African-American and POC clients, and to anyone seeking healing and peace who chooses to build a therapy relationship with me. I'm LGBTQIA+ friendly.

I'm a client-centered therapist. We'll work together to carve out the life you desire.

How I work

Approaches I've found helpful

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT): understanding how thoughts, feelings and actions connect
  • EMDR: certified, for healing from trauma
  • Insight-oriented therapy: making sense of patterns and where they began
  • Client-centered therapy: you lead, I walk alongside you

I'm always growing my practice. I've added written materials to support healing between sessions, and neurofeedback, a brain-training service that calms the nervous system.
